### French numbers 21 - 69

To write numbers from 21 to 69 in French, simply add the unit to the tens.

Connect the two numbers with a hyphen.

vingt (20) + deux (2) = vingt-deux (22)
trente (30) + sept (7) = trente-sept (37)
quarante (40) + neuf (9) = quarante-neuf (49)


*vingt et un* or *vingt-et-un*?

In French, numbers like 21, 31, 41, etc. may appear in two different spellings.

Traditional spelling:

- 21: *vingt et un*
- 31: *trente et un*
- 41: *quarante et un*

According to the traditional rule, the *et* connecting the numbers is not joined by hyphens.

1990 spelling reform:

- 21: *vingt-et-un*
- 31: *trente-et-un*
- 41: *quarante-et-un*

The 1990 reform recommends that multiword numbers be linked entirely with hyphens, including *et*.

Therefore, both *vingt et un* and *vingt-et-un* are correct:

- *vingt et un*: traditional spelling
- *vingt-et-un*: 1990 reform

Both forms are now acceptable. However, to keep spelling consistent, this site prefers the 1990 reform.

::: *note Note*

Not all numbers ending in 1 include *et*:

- 21: *vingt-et-un*
- 31: *trente-et-un*
- 61: *soixante-et-un*
- 71: *soixante-et-onze*
- 81: *quatre-vingt-un*
- 91: *quatre-vingt-onze*

:::
